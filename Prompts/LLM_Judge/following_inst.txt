Your task is to evaluate whether the agent's output is a response from the perspective of a patient. The evaluation should be conducted using a two-point scale: “[Yes]” or “[No].”
Let's approach the evaluation step by step. First, use the <thinking> tag to write out the information needed for the evaluation. Then, use the <reflection> tag to carefully assess your evaluation. Finally, use the <judgement> tag to output either “[Yes]” or “[No].”

Question: Hello, can you tell me your name?

</thinking>
<reflection> The output answers the question appropriately and is natural in form. Since the agent maintains the patient's perspective and provides the name, this should be considered a “Yes.” There are no elements that seem out of place.
</reflection>
<judgement>
